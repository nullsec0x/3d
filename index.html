My Fav Albums

Three albums that had the biggest impact on my music taste.

Stratosphere

Duster

Stratosphere is probably the album that changed my taste the most. Before hearing it I mostly listened to music that was more direct, but this album made me appreciate atmosphere and slower songs. It's the kind of album I can always come back to.

Please Be Nice

Camping In Alaska

I like this album because it feels real. It isn't perfectly produced and that's exactly why I think it works so well.

Epitaph

Necrophagist

Epitaph got me into technical death metal. The musicianship is insane, but the songs are still memorable. It showed me that technical music can still be fun to listen to.
